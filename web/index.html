Employee details

Every employee needs 12 to 100 good photos (front, both sides, chin up / down). You can mix the ways below: upload some photos and capture the rest.

Face-only crops need a border so the face can be found. Auto tries the photo as it is, then adds 35 % / 80 % padding, enlarges small photos and turns rotated ones. A fixed value is tried first, Auto is the fallback.

📁 Way 1 - Upload photos

Select several photos at once (up to 100). Face-only crops, small photos and rotated photos are fixed automatically (padding added, enlarged, turned). Rejected photos show the reason.

👥 Bulk registration - many employees at once

Choose the main folder. Inside it, one folder per employee, named with the employee ID:

all_faces\
 ├─ EMP001\  photos
 ├─ EMP002\  photos
 └─ 1045\    photos

Each employee is checked and saved on its own and needs at least 12 usable photos (up to 100 are used). Face-only crops and small photos get padding (setting above). An existing employee ID is replaced with the new photos.

How to test with a video

  1. Upload the video. A test camera is created in setup mode.
  2. Draw ROI tightly around the walking area. A smaller area = digital zoom = far faces are found better.
  3. Tuning: for CCTV high up / top-down / side view, press Far / top-down / side view.
  4. Register Employee: upload photos, and/or choose the test camera, pause the video and click on the employee's face. Photos from the same camera angle give the best accuracy.
  5. Press Start Live Attendance and Restart the video. Watch Live, then Reports.
  6. Done? Delete all data (top right) removes employees, photos, videos and records.
